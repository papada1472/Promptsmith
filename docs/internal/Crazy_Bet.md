## The Crazy Bet

Most users do not care about prompts.

Prompt engineers do.

Normal users want:

☑ Improve my result.

Not:

☐ Show optimized prompt.

Expert Prompt exists because it helps users gradually think like experts, not because they want to learn prompt engineering.



\## Phase 5 — Portable Personal Context Profile



The floating orb may only be the delivery mechanism.



The real long-term product may become a portable personal context profile.



Instead of users repeatedly externalizing:



\* their writing style,

\* their personality,

\* their business,

\* their frameworks,

\* their audience,

\* their preferences,



Refinzi gradually builds a local-first understanding of the user's working context.



Eventually, users should not think:



"I need to explain this better."



They should think:



"Refinzi already knows how I think."



The shortcut and floating orb remain the interface.



The real product becomes persistent personal context that travels across every AI model and every application.



This is intentionally NOT part of the MVP.



It introduces:



\* privacy concerns,

\* trust requirements,

\* onboarding friction,

\* synchronization problems.



The MVP should first prove that reducing workflow interruption is valuable.



Only after that should Refinzi evolve into a portable personal context layer.
