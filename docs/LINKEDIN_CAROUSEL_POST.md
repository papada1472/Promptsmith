## 🖨️ Step 1: Export Carousel Slides to PDF (60 Seconds)

1. Open [`scripts/linkedin-carousel.html`](file:///e:/Antigravity%20Projects/Refinezy/Refinzi%202.0/scripts/linkedin-carousel.html) in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click the blue **"🖨️ Export PDF for LinkedIn (1080×1350)"** button at the top (or press `Ctrl + P`).
3. In the Chrome/Edge Print dialog:
   - **Destination**: `Save as PDF`
   - **Layout**: `Portrait`
   - **Margins**: `None`
   - **Options**: Check `Background graphics` (Crucial for dark-mode gradients & obsidian glass)
4. Click **Save** as `Refinzi-2.0-Executive-Carousel.pdf`.
5. You now have an ultra-sharp, 8-page 4:5 vertical PDF ready to upload directly to LinkedIn!

## 📈 Pro-Tips to Maximize LinkedIn Algorithm Reach:

1. **Document Posts Have the Highest Dwell Time**:
   LinkedIn’s algorithm ranks posts based on how long users stay on your post. Carousels force users to swipe through 8 slides, which signals extreme quality to the feed.
2. **First 60 Minutes Engagement**:
   - Post between **08:00 AM – 10:30 AM** local time on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.
   - Reply to every single comment within the first hour with an open-ended question to create a threaded discussion.
3. **Drop Links in the Post AND First Comment**:
   Add the GitHub link and `refinzi.com` in both the post body and as the pinned/first comment.
