# Refinzi

## What is Refinzi?

Refinzi helps people improve and add context to any text without leaving the app they're already using.

Simply highlight text anywhere—ChatGPT, Claude, LinkedIn, Gmail, Notion, Word, Slack, or the browser—press a shortcut or tap the floating sparkle, and Refinzi refines it directly where you work.

No browser switching.

No copy-paste workflow.

No prompt engineering.

**Your thoughts, refined with AI.**

**Your AI. Your context. Your workflow.**

---

## The Problem

AI is already intelligent.

The problem is that humans constantly have to interrupt their workflow to externalize additional context, refine wording, or better express what they already know.

Every day, people leave their work simply to:

* make an email clearer,
* rewrite a ChatGPT prompt,
* add one more detail,
* explain their intent better,
* structure their thoughts.

The interruption is the problem.

---

## Our Belief

People do not necessarily want better AI.

People want less interruption.

Refinzi exists to reduce that interruption.

---

## Current ICP Hypothesis

Refinzi is built for AI-native knowledge workers.

People who:

* use AI 20+ times per day,
* frequently rewrite prompts,
* repeatedly add follow-up context,
* want more control over AI outputs,
* hate switching between work and AI chats.

These are the kinds of users who already pay for products like Cursor, Raycast AI, Wispr Flow, and Superhuman.

---

## What Refinzi is NOT

* Not another chatbot.
* Not a prompt marketplace.
* Not a prompt engineering tool.
* Not Grammarly.
* Not a writing assistant.

Refinzi is a workflow layer that sits between the user and AI.
